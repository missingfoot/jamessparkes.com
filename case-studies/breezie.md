# Breezie

## Overview
Breezie was an innovative Android-based operating system designed specifically for elderly and disabled users, focusing on accessibility and ease of use.

## Research & Development
The project involved extensive user research with elderly users, leading to a complete UX/UI overhaul. The design incorporated scalable functionality that could grow with users' comfort levels.

## Components
- Customizable Android OS
- Family management portal
- SmartThings dashboard for care homes
- Device management system
- Training materials and documentation
- Packaging and print materials
