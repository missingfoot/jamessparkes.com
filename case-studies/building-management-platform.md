# Building Management Platform

## Overview
The Building Management Platform was a comprehensive solution combining hotel management, customer relationship management, and financial operations into a single, cohesive system.

## Project Evolution
Starting in 2017, I took on the challenge of transforming an incomplete backend system into a fully-functional management platform. The project underwent multiple iterations, evolving from an internal tool to a viable SaaS solution.

## Key Features
- Integrated booking management system
- Advanced financial tracking and reporting
- Comprehensive analytics dashboard
- Customer relationship management tools
- Staff training and onboarding modules
