# Marketing Website

## Overview
The Marketing Website project involved creating a modern, high-performance website to showcase the company's unique offering and drive sales conversions.

## Design Approach
The website was designed with a focus on user experience, ensuring potential customers could easily understand the company's value proposition and navigate to relevant information. Special attention was paid to the sales funnel optimization.

## Components
- Main marketing website
- Room booking platform
- Events section
- Workspace offerings
- Multiple targeted microsites
- Sales conversion funnels
