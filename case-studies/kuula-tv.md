# Kuula TV

### Overview
Kuula TV is a media platform focused on delivering high-quality video content to viewers while providing content creators with robust management tools.

---

### Design Scope
The project encompassed branding, website design, and the development of a comprehensive media management system. A key focus was creating an intuitive interface for content uploading and organization.

---

### Key Features
- Intuitive content organization system
- Mobile-optimized upload interface
- Basic video editing capabilities
- Course and collection management
- Multi-format content support (video, PDF, audio)
- Partner microsites
