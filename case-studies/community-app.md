# Community App

## Overview
The Community App was designed to enhance resident experience in community-focused apartment buildings, facilitating communication and engagement between residents and staff.

## Development Process
The app was developed through extensive user research and iterative design. Starting with an MVP focused on core features, we continuously expanded functionality based on user feedback and business requirements.

## Feature Set
- Streamlined onboarding experience
- Interest-based community groups
- Direct messaging system
- Events platform
- Help and support system
- Content management system for staff
