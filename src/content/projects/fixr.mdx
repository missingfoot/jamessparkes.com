## Overview

FIXR is a mobile-first ticketing and event management platform empowering event organisers worldwide to sell tickets, manage attendees, and streamline event operations. As a UX designer at FIXR, I worked on various projects ranging from small feature enhancements to larger, headline functionality. Our customer base includes both small, self-serve organisers and larger-scale clients, whose feedback often guided iterative improvements to the product.

One of my most significant projects involved designing a new customer messaging platform to help organisers send notifications, marketing messages, and critical service updates.

## Project: Customer messaging platform

### Project context

To remain competitive and to outperform existing event management competitors, I was tasked with creating a robust messaging platform for FIXR's event organisers. The new platform needed to address three major workflows:

- Sending event notifications
- Dispatching marketing messages
- Delivering critical service updates

### Problem

Prior to this project, organisers could only send basic, single-event notifications—one at a time, with no scheduling options and minimal customisation. This limited their ability to communicate effectively with attendees, especially when promoting multiple events or sending segmented marketing campaigns.

### Goals

<KVGroup>
  <KV term="Scheduling" desc="Enable organisers to schedule messages in advance for better workflow management." />
  <KV term="Multi-event promotion" desc="Allow organisers to promote multiple events simultaneously." />
  <KV term="Customisation & branding" desc="Let organisers use their own copy, tone of voice, and visual branding in messages." />
  <KV term="Service emails & critical updates" desc="Provide a clear path for sending important service messages (e.g., event changes, cancellations)." />
</KVGroup>

### Constraints & considerations

<KVGroup>
  <KV term="Risk of misuse" desc="Granting organisers the power to send potentially thousands of emails or messages at once posed a reputational and compliance risk. We needed to implement checks and safeguards to prevent nefarious or spam-like behaviour." />
  <KV term="Technical feasibility" desc="Creating a scalable messaging system required buy-in from engineering to ensure performance and reliability especially with new added complex systems like timed entry." />
  <KV term="Timeline & scope" desc="We had to deliver a manageable first release that could be iterated on, based on customer feedback." />
</KVGroup>

### Research

<KVGroup>
  <KV term="Competitor analysis" desc="I examined popular CRM tools and direct competitors in the event ticketing space. This helped me understand how others approached messaging workflows, audience segmentation, and brand customisation, as well as how they mitigated misuse (e.g., permissions, throttling, and content guidelines)." />
</KVGroup>

### Stakeholder questioning

<KVGroup>
  <KV term="Accounts team & customer relations" desc="Provided insights into common client requests and pain points." />
  <KV term="Terminology familiarity" desc="Using language and structures familiar to CRM users would reduce the learning curve." />
  <KV term="Audience segmentation" desc="Offering a way to segment attendee groups (e.g., VIPs, repeat customers) was highly desired." />
  <KV term="Robust safeguards" desc="Mechanisms to prevent spam and abuse (e.g., admin approvals, daily send limits for new accounts)." />
</KVGroup>

### Ideation & collaboration

Workshops with stakeholders from various departments revealed what features to potentially include in the MVP:

<KVGroup>
  <KV term="Accounts team" desc="Prioritised must-have features based on demand such as multi-event promotions, scheduling." />
  <KV term="Customer relations" desc="Provided insights into common client requests and pain points and prioritised service updates." />
  <KV term="Engineering" desc="Balanced feasibility and timeline constraints by prioritising features that were most likely to be successful in short term and made designs with additional feature sets for future iterations." />
  <KV term="Product" desc="Prioritised technical requirements like working with other systems such as timed entry." />
</KVGroup>

From these workshops product management created a feature roadmap with clear priorities for the final product where I prioritised and created a prototype from which to get further feedback from the team and stakeholders for an MVP that could be delivered on time by engineering.

<KVGroup>
  <KV term="Low-fidelity wireframes" desc="Started by mapping out user flows for scheduling messages, creating segmented lists, and customising templates." />
  <KV term="Interactive prototypes" desc="Using Figma, built mid- to high-fidelity prototypes to simulate key interactions (e.g., segment selection, scheduling calendar)." />
  <KV term="Feedback rounds" desc="After each iteration, gathered input from internal stakeholders." />
  <KV term="Onboarding" desc="Ensured the interface was straightforward, with step-by-step flows for new users." />
  <KV term="Templates" desc="Added pre-built templates for recurring communications." />
  <KV term="Send safeguards" desc="Implemented sign-posting before sending campaigns, including confirmations and potential send limits for new accounts." />
  <KV term="Scope trimming" desc="Cut certain advanced features (like complex audience segmentation rules) to keep the first release focused and deliverable." />
</KVGroup>

### Outcome & impact

<KVGroup>
  <KV term="Improved workflow efficiency" desc="Organisers could schedule and send multiple notifications in one go, saving significant time." />
  <KV term="Brand consistency" desc="Users now had the freedom to include custom branding and maintain their own tone of voice." />
  <KV term="Risk management" desc="Built-in checks reduced the chance of spam or misuse, preserving FIXR's reputation." />
  <KV term="Positive feedback" desc="Beta testers praised the intuitive interface and welcomed the ability to reach their attendees in a more personalised, scalable way." />
</KVGroup>

Although there was no official KPI measurement at the time of initial launch, anecdotal feedback from organisers indicated they experienced higher open rates and better engagement when promoting events through the new messaging system.
