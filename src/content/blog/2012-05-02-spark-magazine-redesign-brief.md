Spark is a new addition to store shelves, however it is struggling to make headway among other high end magazines in our target market. Our audience is very style conscious and we need to convey this through the design of our brand’s image in order to effectively communicate our primary values and what Spark should mean to our readers.

The current image of Spark magazine in the UK is not inline with other brands such as Wired and GQ whom we are competing with on the shelves. This brand redesign will be a direct consequence of marketing research we have conducted that shows the price of the magazine does not correlate to the aesthetic quality we should be aspiring to.

You are tasked with producing an entirely redesigned Spark magazine which stands out from the digital version of the magazine and that effectively communicates the magazine’s main goal of providing a collection of the inspiring ideas and stories that will captivate our readers.
