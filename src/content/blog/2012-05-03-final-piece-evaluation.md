I was finally able to get a good majority of the modelling done. However I had a lot of problems with adding materials. Houdini would instantly crash whenever I tried making any so I resorted to using simple UV projects that at least gave my geometry some sort of texture to them. This was not ideal as it resulted in low quality looking results with the texture stretched over the terrain or objects making it look like a crap Nintendo 64 game. I probably would have been better off making it in Minecraft.

Since my project was a day late I simply had to get it rendered of as quickly as possible unfinished. I was also unable to make the humanoid statues at the sides of the mountains and instead opted for a much quicker castle on a procedurally modelled rocky island. Once I had haphazardly added some UV projects top the geometry I then rendered it at a low 720p resolution to be brought into Photoshop.

Once in Photoshop I added better texturing such as trees and rock to parts of the scene. This was made easier by having each object rendered out separately with an alpha channel. I could quickly and easily use layer masks to paint in where I wanted the textures to appear, sweeping the poor attempt to texture in Houdini under the carpet. Overall I feel due to not being able to pin down an idea for my project soon enough I’d left it too late so my other projects took a higher priority over it as I’d been having trouble with Houdini for the duration of the project and kept putting it off.

This was especially disastrous as it meant I left the project to the final week before it needed to be handed in with far too many problems still unresolved making the progress of the actual project painfully slow and with subpar results. I feel that I could have handled the situation much better if I had made sure I had an adequate idea of what I wanted to do much earlier in the term.
