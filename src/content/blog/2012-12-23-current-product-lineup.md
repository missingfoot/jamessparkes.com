Here I have listed all the current products on the Archos store to help me visualise what kind of products they produce and at what price ranges. From this I can see their main products are tablets and media players which are much like iPod Touches and iPod Nanos.

However in 2010 and 2011 they produced netbooks and laptops, it appears they have this year stopped doing so, however I would very much like laptops to be part of my lineup of products for my rebrand of Archos.

I will need to consolidate the plethora of products they sell into easy to distinguished categories and classes, at the moment it is a giant shit storm of products at the same produce range in the same class and with hardly any difference at all.

I would like to introduce a clear hierarchy of products that will not confuse the customer but will give them a clear and concise way of choosing what is right for them and not saturating the market with indistinguishable gadgets. Also, looking at the list of products they sell, I have absolutely no idea what any of them are. Their names bare no resemblance to what are are or what they do, I would need to come up with a better naming structure so that I can tell what it is from the name and price alone.

At the moment their priorities appear to lie in Android tablets and media players, with a dash of eReaders. I would like to keep tablets as the focus but move media players out of the spotlight and have a focus on ultrabooks and possibly even professional laptops as these markets are currently very popular compared to the waning media player market which is currently stagnated due to smartphones.

Another product area I feel could be expanded on is the smartphone, it is clear they have made a start with an Android phone, however I think this can be taken all the way to making a fully fledged smartphone to compete with the market leaders of today.
