In order to move on I will need to establish the brand values which will make the new Archos distinct from competitors such as Google, Amazon and Apple. I had also established I wanted the company to exude a sense of friendliness but also from my original brief, aspiration as well. From this I have recently been developing what is to become the brand values of the rebranded Archos, these include:

**Innovative** – I want to get across that Archos comes up with things and bring them to market usually much sooner than the bigger players, this is one of their strongest aspects and the branding and advertising should reflect this. In the brand guidelines I am working on at the moment I want the readers of this to realise this so it comes through in their marketing of products and designing of future campaigns by other designers or contracted design agencies.

**Aspirational** – I want to in some way show that Archos is aspirational and thus their audience should be as well, and that by choosing Archos they are spiring to become batter or better their life in some way by using Archos’ products. Tablet computing has revolutionised the way people do things, so instead of saying how revolutionary the product is, why not suggest how much the consumer’s lives will be revolutionised?

**Daring** – Again, going back to Archos bringing many different and new things to market first, they are one of the only major tablet manufacturers who deliver stock Android, they make interesting TV remote interfacing devices and put Android in alarm clocks, pretty crazy stuff compared to other companies. So why not convey how daring they are?

**Caring** – A lot of the negativity and criticism I found when reading articles and comments on tech news websites and blogs is that their products are cheap and break easily. One way of changing this perception is to convey quality but also convey that they care about their customers through after-sales suport so that people trust them more to invest in their products and thus have a better perception of them in the long run.

**Helpful** – Realting back to the previous point, they need to come across as being helpful to the users who already own their products, this can be done in documentation such as manuals and on their website and such so that they feel like the company is still interacting with them in a way that they don’t just see them as being expendable.

**Cheerful** – Through the tone of voice used in their material such as advertising audiences should be able to pick up on the playful nature of the new Archos, this should help establish a connection with existing and future customers and users of their products so that they are retained.

**Solid** – At the moment people buy their products, then usually move on to another brand. There is no staying power, the previous points should combat that but the idea of being a solid product will ‘solidify’ people’s trust in the brand and products. People won’t feel the need to move on to an iPad or Nexus 7 if they feel Archos is just as solid in terms of quality and usability as the others.

I will use these principles when putting together the brand guidelines which I will need to continue producing any materials. This will help me keep a consistent theme across all of the things i will be producing.
