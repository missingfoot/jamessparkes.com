*   Publications – Advertising campaign examples (posters, magazine adverts)
*   Corporate Documents – Internal editorial design (reports)
*   Online Presence – website design
*   Self promotion – Stationary, corporate business cards, merchandise
*   Product design – 3D renders of products (either through learning Maya or commissioning some 3D renders, which ever the schedule and budget dictates)
