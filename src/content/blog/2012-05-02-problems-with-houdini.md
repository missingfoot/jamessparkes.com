As well documented at uni I’ve been having a lot of issues regarding my Houdini installation. At first I couldn’t even use Houdini on my computer die to X11 not being installed, and it took quite some time to discover this was the issue thanks to asking on the Side FX forum. Once this had been sorted out however it wasn’t long until I was instructed to use Houdini 12 due to my serial number being upgraded since it was bought within 9 days of the upgrade. This posed many issues with the computability of my files.

When attempting to make any progress with my project I would always be presented with fatal errors and my file being recovered with nothing in it. This meant I had to incrementally add things to my scene and save, duplicate the file every time I did anything to avoid losing my progress. After a while it occurred to me that this was a problem with version 12 of the software and that I should use 11, however is posed even more problems as I couldn’t get my 12 files to work with 11 or vice versa. I ended up have a whole bunch of files that worked in neither version of the software.
