I found the anual report on of the ost difficult and challenging parts of the project. This was mostly due to my experience with the document. I really should have done more research into them and figured out exactly the narrative of them as for the most part I was trying to figure it out by looking at other reports. My initial ideas for the annual report was to have a book which had infographics, charts and other various interesting information in it. But i found it very difficult to cultivate this into a way which made sense in the context.

I didn’t have any information on Archos, besides very little from a French finical statement they release. I decided upon using the two HTC annual reports as a base, taking their overal structure and information and then builkding the Archos brand around that so at least I had something to work with. I came across problems when looking for ststisitics. I could have made them upo but then, how would I have justified them and where would they go? The reports I was wokring off did not have these.

I feel that if i was working closly with a small company, I could have gleaned this information fromt hem personally and could have producted somethign with relevance and coherance, but working totally in the dark with Archos I found it very difficult. I have certianly learned that I can’t dive into something as large scale as an annual report without much initial research. I assumed that by looking at well desinged ones on Behance would be enough, but really it’s the content which threw me. I do feel now after the project that I have a lot more knowldge now than when I started, but a lot fo time was spent on doing research at the wrong time. I was doing it during the making, as opsoed to before.

These factors mean that it is probably the weakest part of my overal rebrand materials, however the lessons I learnt from it are probably amongst the most valuable.
