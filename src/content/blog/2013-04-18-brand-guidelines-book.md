I have been designing the grid and layout which I will be using for all my documents. This should allow for a consistent look across all materials I will be producing and is especially important due to the tag logo I have developed as the midpoint of the replies on the margin, so having the margin be the same across everything is important so that the logo is always the same size and proportions, at least on A4 paper.

I wanted the brand guidelines to act as a showcase for all the various elements which make up the brand of Archos, the tone, the narrative, the logos. I wrote it in the style of being light hearted and friendly so that this is picked up by the readers of the book so they transfer those values into their design or marketing work.

I also took inspiration from the Mediacom book in that they have everything sparsely laid out. I liked this approach and decided to do my own take on it, I had my grid and places the text in the centre of the page leaving lots of white space, so that when in the hands of readers they are being taken on a journey where they read one thing, process it and pause to let it sink in, then continue onto the next page.

If I had the opportunity to do the book again I would certainly have developed the concept of the friendly faces more. Perhaps had them in more parts, at the moment looking back at the finished book I think that they are too few to make an impact and to justify their existence. However I am glad with the brand guidlines and I feel they convey the ideas I had about the brand well and it serves it’s purpose, which is to guide other designers working with the Archos brand to keep the image consistent and to its point.
