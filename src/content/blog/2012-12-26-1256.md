Since ‘Archos’ is apparently Greek for ‘master’, I have decided to look at some Greek gods and mythological figures for inspiration on some names I could perhaps use for products so that each type has an easy and clear name. Some of the ones I liked included:

*   Eos
*   Atlas
*   Ares
*   Orion
*   Aurora (Roman, but it’s still cool)
*   Nemesis
*   Hydros
*   Acis
