I have been spending some time looking into how I can consolidate the product ranges for my version of Archos. I really would like to take the route that Vizio took and break into the computing market and offer notebooks as well as portable devices. I have carefully designed a product hierarchy that I have attached rough pricing for to get an idea of how many types of products and how many 3D models will be necessary.

For the advertising and promotional material I will be making I won’t be needing the media players since I’d rather focus on the new product type, the notebooks, and also their primary product, the tablets and even a phone.
