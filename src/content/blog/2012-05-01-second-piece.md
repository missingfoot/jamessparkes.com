For my second piece I want to explore a style of digital art that is apparently commercially viable such as the abstract and vibrant composites I often see in being used in advertisements. They the style seems to be rather popular with companies who want to apply it to their products, so I think that going down that route would be beneficial to me.

In my previous attempt at abstract it didn’t pan out very well and I was unhappy with the results, however I have given myself more time to come up with a concept and work on it. I still think that abstract is my weak point but I need to be diverse and abstract is something I see a lot of in the real world so I defiantly want to get familiar with it technically.

I still am struggling with coming up with ideas and so I have been looking at a wide array of other people’s work in order to get some ideas and to narrow down a route I want to take.

pieces

From this I can begin to figure out what type of composition I want to do and based on what I’m already doing I know what I can’t do already. Doing something that would heavily rely on the style I’m already doing for my other piece, the matte painting, would not be ideal as I want to differentiate the two styles and techniques in order to fulfil the brief.
