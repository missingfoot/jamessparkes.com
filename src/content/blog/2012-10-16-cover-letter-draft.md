I’ve recently been trying to put together a cover letter, and based on some research I feel like I have made some progress. It doesn’t feel perfect at the moment and the is still a lot of room for improvement, it would be helpful to have some feedback before I go any further with it.

I spent time reading tips and advice on how to write a good cover letter including a few articles, one of which from Smashing Magazine has some useful things that employers had said they either like or don’t like to see in cover letters which was helpful. Overall it seems that most advice was pointing towards something casual and short, something that explained who they were and what they want and to not sound like you has copy and pasted something.

It was clear that since I would be applying for creative roles I couldn’t use a stuffy business tone as advised from the articles, so I decided to go for a more natural flow like it was me, a person, speaking to them on a personal level. Crucially I need to treat them as individuals and to show that I have taken a genuine interest in them, otherwise, why would they spend time reading my letter if I didn’t even bother to spend the time to get to know their agency or company and to see if I was even a good fit for them?

I decided to start short and to the point and this is what I have so far:

> Hello **\[NAME OF PERSON\]**
> 
> I’m a graphic design student (about to be) fresh out of university and with about 4 years of experience in the field both as a part time employee with a small company and doing some freelance work in range of disciplines such as branding, advertising, print and web design.
> 
> I’ve looked at your company’s portfolio and I’m seriously impressed with the kind of work you do. I’d love to become part of your team and to work with you on the awesome stuff you do, bringing my talent and skills which I think would really compliment your own style and design principles.
> 
> If you’d like to know more about me you can always find my info and portfolio at [sprk.es](http://sprk.es/)
> 
> And even if you don’t think I’m what you’re looking for, it would be cool if you guys could give me any advice or thoughts on me or my work!
> 
> Anyway, thank you for the time.
> 
> James Sparkes
