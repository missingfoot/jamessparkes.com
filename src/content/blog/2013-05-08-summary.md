I have found this  class in particular to be one of the best we have had at uni, I felt like a learnt a great deal as opposed to just doing a big project, it reminded me of the second year and photo manipulation and 3D when the class was more interactive with each other and we learnt new skills and theory. This was evident in the fun and interesting workshops we had, they were the sort of thing I had been waiting for and there should have been more throughout the entire degree and not just this year.

The other thing I very much enjoyed was the whole atmosphere of working in a group, when the deadline loomed the tension rocketed and everyone was hard at work doing their thing, it felt almost like a real working environment, and if that it what it’s like in real life then I believe I will thoroughly enjoy that. I even decided to be a bit risky an emply a new art style I had never even tried before and it paid off, (the one with the rocket in the cut-out cartoon style) being my favourite piece of work I contributed towards the book I now have a type of work which I can show off in my portfolio which I have always wanted but never had the appropriate opportunity.

I found this class very helpful as it increased my confidence in working with other people and conveying my ideas and also it was very fun to work on a major project which was being printed as a physical form to own and hold, as opposed to submitting a digital file or evern getting it printed at uni which isn’t nearly anything like working with a real printer. Many new things were learnt and experiences had during Contemporary Graphic Design, and the final outcome is one I am very proud of. It’s awfully good to have a tactile object to hold after all the work and effort everyone put into it, and having been a part of it and seeing first hand what everyone did adds to the experience greatly.

I also felt that the return of critiques were very good as I need someone to tell me if something is wrong or right, what is wrong or right and other ideas and suggestions so that i can deduce what it is I can fix or make better about my work. Working alongside the group was great for that as everyone always had something to say about either my work or someone else’s  I also enjoyed critiquing and helping others work which I found rewarding as it meant I am a lot more confident in my own thoughts and skills to communicate those things.

I now plan on getting a junior designer job, hoping my university education combined with my 4 years work experience will be enough to bypass the internship stage. However a lot of places do say they’d prefer experience working in an agency so I will try to get a junior designer job first, and if it becomes apparent that I am unsuccessful I will then fall back on applying for internships to get that agency experience. I have already started applying for work in agencies I find genuinely interesting places that I’d like to work. I am also thinking about freelance work, perhaps even setting up an agency of my own, however this is long term stuff and I would prefer to have a few years getting experience in a stable career first before making a decision on that. I can see a significant change in both myself and my skills, especially looking back at the beginning of this degree which has taught man valuable lessons.

The End
