I have been compiling some notes for use in my CV, I started by describing my work and university experience and listed skills I either have or things I have done in the past. Depending on the specific position I will be applying for I would customise the CV’s contents to reflect the particular skills the offer wants as to not be too irrelevant. Here are a few things I have so far:

**Work Experience**

Currently working for a small product design company as their in-house designer, producing a wide range of print based and digital material, from branding, packaging, identity, advertising, to web design.

Had the opportunity to work with an array of people from different countries and have handled a diverse range of design problems in various fields, even ones I had no previous experience in.

Also various freelance projects in branding and identity such as app developers creating logos and icons, also some UI design for the apps and corresponding websites.

**Education**

Majored in graphic design with photo manipulation and 3D minors. Created advertising campaigns in group projects as well and magazine rebranding and redesign. Also large scale personal projects in digital art such as film poster design and matte painting landscapes in photo manipulation and 3D.
