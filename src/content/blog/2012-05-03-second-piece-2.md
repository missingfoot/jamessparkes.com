After looking at other people’s work to see what could influence me and what design elements I could bring across into my work I decided to then start looking for stock. I new that I wanted to use a human figure as the main focal point so went to look for some good model stock on devianART, specifically mjranum-stock’s work as it is some of the best quality on devianART.

Once I had found a stock model that I thought would be good to work with I started getting ideas of where to take the piece further. I knew from the other pieces I looked at I wanted to have some sort of star-centric theme as it is a style I like but also something that looks attractive. I think it would be interesting to see how I can blend a space/star theme with the relatively light colour scheme I had borrowed from the Anoraak album cover.

I then set about trying to build up a sort of solar system diorama that that figure would be shielding from the light of and reaching up for. I used the existing lighting on the figure as the centre of the diorama with the sun and concentric circles around it with planets casting shadows into the scene. I used mostly NASA images as they are in the public domain which made looking for additional stock imagery much easier as I didn’t have to worry about the rules or copyright issues.

Once I had the rough placement for the elements i started to clean up the diorama by giving the planets their lighting so it looked natural. I accomplished this by using layer masks and taking away where the planet should be cast in shadow leaving behind the colour of the background but also taking away the stars to show they are in the background and the absence of anything in the foreground is that shadow.

However I do not feel that I had the second piece completed to the standard that I initially had wanted. The solar system feels unfinished and I wanted to get in a few more planets, however time was already running out. I think if I had given even more time I could improved the second piece further, perhaps giving more attention to the figure and exploring skin touchups and enhancements we had covered. Overall I’m happy with how it turned out even though I don’t feel it’s fully there, but it is undoubtably an imprivment on my previous attempt at something abstract.
