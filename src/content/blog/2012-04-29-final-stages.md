During the final stages of the matte painting it was becoming almost impossible to work with the PSD file as it was getting over a gigabyte, and I hadn’t even used any smart objects like I normally do. This was all down to the huge resolution I was working at and the enormous amount of layers I needed. I think it was the trees and the many layers of road surfaces I had used.

For the final few elements, such as the buildings and the distant background I had to do these in separate documents using a JPEG of my unfinished scene so that I had the positioning correct, I simply needed to bring them over into the main document once they were complete. I had started work on the buildings, something I was going to use to add additional framing to my piece and also to show the vast time scales the scene was to convey.

I wanted to have a few tall skyscrapers poking out from the vegetation and for them to look extremely deteriorated. To accomplish this I begun looking for photos of tall skyscrapers and buildings that were not only the correct perspective, which was difficult enough since not many people take photos of skyscrapers from the a nice front of perspective (most are from up close and below) but they needed to not look new and shiny with glass covering them.

This was an interesting problem and the solution was to look for buildings already under construction. This ensured that the buildings were modern, but the process of making them look neglected would be a lot easier as they didn’t have windows, or only a few windows, but they were also unfinished at the top, so it looked like they had fallen apart slowly. This made a few things possible. First of all I could overlay various textures such as old cracked concrete, rubble, and moss textures, and using layer masks could paint in where I wanted the textures to show and I could manipulate their structure as they were nicely from a front on perspective.

For one of the buildings, I took the aspect of the floors caving in and the building being all crooked from the Ruin short and applied this to one of the buildings that didn’t look like it was mostly made of steal. I used a predominantly concrete looking skyscraper and cut it up into sections, then I rotated them slightly and blended them back together and adding in some other images of rocky textures to get the look of the floors collapsing in on each other.

Once all these elements where completed I dragged them over into the main document and positioned them correctly based on their perspective and size so that there is a good composition. I will now need to do something about the vanishing point protruding from the road as this is proving to be the most difficult part of the work, I still haven;t come up with an elegant solution of how to deal with the road blending in with the background as of yet.
