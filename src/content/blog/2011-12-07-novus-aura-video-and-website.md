Here is our group’s final piece of brand communications in the form of a short animation created by Rich that would reside on the fake website for the design collective found here. I feel that the overal branding of the hypothetical motion graphics company we initially envisioned at the start of the process comes across effectively, with all the material tying in together, from the logo to the text and wording that was used to the animation.

<a class="video-embed" data-provider="vimeo" data-id="33286891" href="https://vimeo.com/33286891">Play video</a>

  
In order to bring across the intentions of our design collective we decided to make a basic website, with the logo, the collective’s values and a few examples of ‘our work’ that we theoretically could have made if it were a real videography agency.

We picked out a few good examples of videos that incorporated skills that our team members possessed, with Rich focusing on filming, editing and motion graphics and Tia also on motion graphics. I however was mostly focusing on the graphic design aspect, therefore we used videos which contained all of these skills to show what we envision the collective accomplishing if it were an established agency. We looked at imaginative conceptual videos and music videos as well as more traditional works.

I felt that displaying all this information in one place such as a website would be more effective in getting across a broader message of what we wanted the collective to communicate. The goal was to create a company that specialised in videography and motion graphics and we had to make sure that the values we decided on came across in the branding in order to create a consistent identity that people can recognise and would communicate effectively to the type of clients we would want to work with which I think it does successfully.
