So what exactly is nuclear power anyway? Basically it’s a term used to describe the use of controlled nuclear fission to generate heat and energy. Essentially it’s a giant kettle heating water and turning it into steam which gets fed into a turbine, generating power.

It’s apparent many people know the negative side of nuclear power, but what are the good things about it? What does it do other forms of energy production can’t provide? In my research I have discovered the following positive points about nuclear power and what it can do for us:

*   Nuclear fuel is extremely abundant and produces huge amounts of energy compared to conventional fossil fuels and renewable energy.

*   Renewable energy solutions are not enough to completely solve global warming as they are too unreliable, expensive and inefficient.

*   Advancements in technology have solved most of the problems regarding nuclear waste, accidents and decommissioning.

*   Contrary to popular belief, nuclear power is one of the safest ways of producing high quantities of energy with the smallest impact on the environment.

*   And nuclear power is the closest thing so far to a fully clean way of producing energy for everyone regardless of their location.

*   The costs involved in nuclear power a much lower than that of fossil fuels and renewable energy solutions.

The fear and rejection of nuclear power plants and their destructive impact on society and the environment is highly exaggerated, especially in the media. But when you look into the fine details you’ll find another side to the argument.

And remember, if you’re against using nuclear power then you’re against using sunlight.
