I needed to show how the packaging would look for the rebranded Archos. I went with producing packaging for the Platinum range of tablets I had creating the advertiing campaign for. I wanted to keep the box design neutral as I was aware that a specifically advertising campaign might not outlast the shelf life of the products themselves, so couldn’t feature any imagery of the carina campaign.

I also employed the concept of using the different colours to represent the different models, for instance, the lime green represents the top of the line model, the pink represents the middle range and then the cooler blue colour represents the lowest end model. This will also be practical in retail environments as each one can be easily identifiable as the sides of the boxes will show those colours.

The box colour is also dependant on the colour of the actual tablet. For instance, I have used a black tablet for the middle model, meaning that the background used is white, compared to the other tablets which are white. Essentially  whatever the colour of the tablet, the negative is used for the box background  giving a good contrast in which to show the product off on. The same concept could be used for adverting posters.
