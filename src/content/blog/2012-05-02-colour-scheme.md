I was listening to a song called Above Your Head by Anoraak, the song itself and subsequently the look and feel of the album cover was a big influence in what I imagined when I was thinking about the theme and concept of my second piece. I really like that cover art and wanted to base my colour scheme on it.

I took various samples of the colours then created a gradient from it. I then used a large white soft brush set to overlay to gradually building up the brightness in the bottom half of the gradient to replicate the photograph used for their album artwork.
