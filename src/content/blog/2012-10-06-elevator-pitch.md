Today we were tasked with coming up with an elevator pitch, a short and to the point description of ourselves that we can tell potentional emolyees and to help us with our cover letter and CV later on. It was an interesting exersise trying to come up with words to describe myself and some pieces of my work.

Throughout the class a few tips I had picked up on was when describing the concept behind our work we needed to be very positive about the way it was conceived and to try and give it a specific purpose as to why I made it as opposed to simply stating it was a class assignment which is passive, we were told to be more assertive of what it was we had created ad why it matters to employers.

For instance, my photo manipulation work from last year, among the best I think I did, was rather personal and there wasn’t much of a brief besides ‘create something in a style I like’, so I need to think how I can turn that around and explain it’s commercial value. The entire point of my final piece was to gain experience in developing a large scale matte painting which has it’s value in the film and video game industry so I can talk about that when explaining my work.

The other piece of work I chose was a bit easier to explain, which was the rebrand of a magazine brief, and since I wrote the brief myself it would be a lot easier to describe the value in it for potential employers as I can discuss the process of redesigning their branding image to better suit their target audience.

A few of the pointers we were to work towards included:

*   Keep it short & have a hook & Pitch yourself
*   What type of work you do / want to do
*   What are your characteristics / qualifications / skills / experience
*   Showcase your 2 best pieces of work, explain the concept and how you met the brief

So far, the working draft is as follows:

> I’m James, I’m really into digital art, branding and UI design you know. I’ve had a few years experience working in a small company doing corporate design involving graphic design for print, web design and photo manipulation. I’ve also got a degree in digital design from university and I have some seriously mad software skills, Adobe certified and everything.
