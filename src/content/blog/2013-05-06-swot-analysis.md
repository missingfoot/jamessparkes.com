**Strengths**  
During the initial stages of the process I was surprised by how outspoken I had found myself during meetings and discussions  Once I discovered that I was part of the book teams I found it very helpful to ask as much as I could about the book so that I could be on the same level as everyone else. I usually find it very difficult to speak up in group environments but within this task I believe contributed as much as most other people as opposed to sitting there afraid to bring forward ideas, significantly boosting confidence in similar situations elsewhere. I also found it surprising easy to convey my ideas however vague or cryptic they might be someone else in the group understood to a certain degree, strengthening the ideas I was contributing. Another aspect I thought was very helpful was the fact that being a group of so many with many different interests, the ideas were extremely varied and many concepts I would have never considered by myself were explored because of the diverse range of experiences of everyone else.

**Weaknesses**  
One of the weaker moments was definitely after all the meetings, over the month long holiday when we were all expected to produce the spreads. I found this personally very difficult as this was when I think group interaction would have been at one of it’s most vital moments. I was unable to get going since I was concerned about doing someting ‘wrong’ which caused me to delay and procrastinate until the very end of the holiday. We had the Facebook group to post our sketches to but I think having meetings during this time would have been much better and the amount of time it took to actually produce the spreads could have been drastically reduced. For the next week or two everyone’s spreads were still being finalised and refined all the way up until the deadline. The month before hand could have been utilised better, and communication could have been increased. Especially when putting together external collaborator content, the communication during this aspect of the production seemed a bit lacking and I found myself confused until getting clarification on the matter.

**Opportunities**  
The fact that some people did not show up to meetings and did not contribute as much as other people I thought in a way was a good thing, this meant the overal size of the group was reduced to only those who were genuinely interested about the project were more heavily involved and that gave the opportunity to work together in a much more efficient way. It was good having a lot of people to product content but when there were too many people all doing the same thing it got a bit messy. When the groups were small they felt much more focused and things got done. Also being part of a team producing a real book was a very good opportunity to see first hand all the stages it takes to bring a publication to print, covering all the bases and being a part of that was highly useful, especially in terms of experience which I can refer back to for later on in career situations.

**Threats**  
I initially did not like the idea of the university having a part to play in the book, when discussing funding it was suggested they could use it as a vehicle for publicity which could have compromised the value and integrity of the book being our production. Thankfully this never came to light and the university was not intrusive and also provided adequate funding for the book. However this did bring forward interesting questions which could in other situation threaten a project, what happens when an external force says they will fund something but if they can make changes to your idea? How do you handle that? This was a valuable lesson I feel, one that I will probably nee dto consider in the future as well with other projects. Another threat was seen in the pricing and the lack of communication between us and the people who would be funding it. It would have been helpful to get a solid budet earlier on and also the problem with the printer and the misquote meaning the size had to be reduced, however the size ended up being very good in the end, probably a better size than the initial dimensions, however this could easily have not been the case which is why I will certainly take away from this experience.
