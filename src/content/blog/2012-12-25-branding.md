I decided to look further into branding as it is the primary focus of my project and pick up some crucial understanding of what it really is and how to utilise it and then apply that to my rebrand of Archos. From what I learnt, a brand can be described as being a name, design or symbol etc that is distinct and stands out as having values which people can identify with. This is then established and communicated through marketing which is essentially building relationships and trust with their audience.

Brands communicate to their audiences through visual messaging and experience, employing sensory elements like how something looks, it’s colours the style or how something feels, the materials used, or other means depending on what they produce. It’s apparent that the experience the audience have with the brand is the most important thing and that they utilise communications with visual awareness or things like slogans and tags lines that summarise their brand values.

Other companies may sell the same thing but the key is having a brand that has a distinct message that it communicates with the audience, for instance, even though Apple is only one company of many that sell computers, phones and tablets, they have a very strong and robust image that sets them apart from other companies like Hewlett Packard or Dell. People associate them with user experience, minimal design and innovation because of how they convey themselves through their branding.

It seems that the key is how a brand is ‘positioned’ in the market places, which is how the audience would perceive a certain brand as being individual and distinct within whatever sector they are in, and in my case that would be the very competitive consumer electronics areas. I would really need to consider how I would want the audience to think and feel about the products they produce and what their perception of the company itself is.

Some helpful questions I will need to answer before making more progress include things like:

**What is Archos become?**  
Archos will be the gap between cheap and low quality and expensive and high quality. There needs to be someone in the middle that can offer fantastic design aesthetic and user experience but not be a luxury brand out of reach of the average user. Why do cheaper electronics need to be ugly and poorly designed? Archos will be the answer to that question, a premium product for a realistic and comfortable price.

**What will I want to achieve with the new rebranded Archos?**  
They will offer a non-confusing collection of phones, tablets, eReaders and notebook computers for a comfortable price and deliver a media consumption service to rival things like Virgin Media, Apple TV, Roku and other content platforms and work seamlessly across all devices, providing people with a very user friendly experience.

**Who is the target market now and who will they be after?**  
Looking at their current product ranges I felt extremely confused by what they were selling and who to, they had very low price points and many many products all the the exact same category leading me to believe their audience were the ones who are settling for something cheap.

However I want this to turn around and address the consumer who feels overwhelmed by countless products and consolidate each product type into an obvious entity that will rival the top players in the current market through a low price point and good aesthetics.
