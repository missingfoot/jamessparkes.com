A fascinating look at why videos on the internet can randomly go viral from a TED talk with a guy called Kevin Allocca, a YouTube ‘trends manager’. He looks at how things which start off as silly arbitrary videos with little meaning can suddenly explode into a huge creative outlet, allowing anyone to participate from Nyan cat remixes to parodies of Rebecca Black’s Friday.
