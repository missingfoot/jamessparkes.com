After careful deliberation and research into my company ideas, I have decided to go with a rebrand of Archos. I feel this will be the strongest out of my ideas as I have a keen interest and keep up with tech news and would really want something like that in my portfolio. I also feel it will be challenging without it being something I am not used to at all, there will be a few things that I have no previous experience in practically but are things I have always wanted to do, so this seems like a good excuse to put those ideas into practice.

I feel that Archos as a company is a very good example of a company that is in need of image readjustment. As someone who is into technology and reads about it in the news, Archos is a company that has never really come up much over recent years. Their consumer awareness is dire having asked many people if they have heard of them and all answers turning up as no. They also have had a long history in hand held tablet devices, a part of the tech market booming right now, yet they seem to be the slowest to react of all the companies.

I feel that their image and product line is weak enough that I can come in and identify many areas that can be changed or improved which will make writing my brief much easier, especially considering interest in the general subject area. I think it will be very interesting to see where I can take the companies branding and what marketing ideas I could explore in improving its image and place in the market.
