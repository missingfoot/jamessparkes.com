For some time now I have been looking into various products and forms of media, in order to come to a conclusion on what my subject will be and what, within that, I will focus on and explore. Initially I thought about doing a film, Battle Royale specially, due to extensive subtexts and deeper meanings that can be extracted.

I assumed this would be a good choice, however upon having a meeting with Alan, he suggested that writing about an entire film would too broad of a subject, and that I needed to focus on something smaller and condensed, he suggested a trailer for a film or perhaps the business model of iTunes and went on to show me a political advertisement video on YouTube which helped me a great deal in terms of where to look for meaning that was not apparent. Some of the various texts I contemplated looking at include:

*   Trailer for film—perhaps a comedy or action film where they show the majority of the funniest or exhilarating scenes—leaving the audience with a final product that doesn’t live up to the original promises of the advertisement

*   Television Advert—something that will be very short and concise where the focus would be very defined

*   The News—and the way they use certain language to influence the audience’s views of a particular topic

*   iTunes—and it’s walled-garden which doesn’t allow much freedom when you want to go outside it—but within it—it is vast and fruitful as long as you stay committed to Apple’s ecosystem

*   Gizmodo—a technology website blog about consumer electronics and technology owned by Gawker Media

*   YouTube Video—something similar to the Lithuanian mayor video which has strong political subtexts and rhetorics

*   Charlie Brooker Article—frank and insightful observations with a strong rhetoric behind them

*   Apple products—going from being a laughing stock in the industry to the most desired products on the technology market in almost all categories

After looking at all of these in detail, I decided that I didn’t want to do these, for instance, I have looked at Apple products, specifically the iPad, before in a previous essay, so I didn’t want to repeat the same theme of going straight to Apple products or iTunes. It is also a very obvious choice, something I didn’t want to make. However I felt I should stay in the area off technology since it’s a topic I am interested in.

I was reading through articles, looking for ideas when I came across one which talked about the Amazon Kindle and it’s dominance in the ebook market. This got me thinking about how I could look into how they have used rhetorics as they clearly did something right. After doing some research to make sure that it was something I felt I could deconstruct sufficiently I watched some of their advertisements and read articles to see how people view the Kindle.

Making sure that I could answer the questions:

*   What is it attempting to persuade the reader or viewer to agree with?
*   What are some of the ways that it attempts to do so?
*   How does it attempt to stop people from doing something different?

The answers to these questions were clear when deconstructing the Kindle’s advertisements which was something I couldn’t so easily do with other ideas I had.
