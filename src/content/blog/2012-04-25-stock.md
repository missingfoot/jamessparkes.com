Once I had a very good idea for my piece I then needed stock as this would make or break the entire concept depending on what I could find. I had a few good ideas of what I was looking for, either a closed in street with high-rise buildings on either side or some sort of highway or overpass with skyscrapers.

I looked through the usual stock websites such as deviantART’s resource section and Fotolia due to it’s relatively low prices. There were a few candidates I found on deviantART however as good as the composition of the photo was their quality was just not high enough. Either there was too much to do to it to get it up to the standards I wanted or it simply was not big enough to fill the huge size of the A3 resolution.

I found this particularly difficult as I needed just one very high resolution image to serve as my base image. I decided to look on premium websites when I came across the perfect photo on Fotolia of an overpass. I immediately looked at it and could envision what I wanted to do with it. I mostly wanted the road and overpass and the rest of the image was mostly flat and barren meaning it was almost a blank canvas for me to add whatever I wanted such as trees and buildings.

This image I felt was composed very well and I could easily see where I could begin to make changes to the landscape to conform to what I had in mind. I looked at the photo for some time before beginning, mentally planning out how I would go about doing anything, such as: how will I make the roads look weathered, how would I add trees realistically, how would I make things look damaged, how can I make it look like it’s situated in a city etc.

After a while of this I felt I had come to an overall good conclusion and I finally began working on it. I started off by gathering some more stock, such as textures that I could use to make surfaces look old much like what I had seen in the works which I had looked at earlier.
